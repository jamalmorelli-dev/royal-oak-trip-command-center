DETROIT TRIP — PRINTING RULES

FLIGHT
- You do NOT need to print flight documents yourself.
- Air France confirms online check-in succeeded but could not issue the boarding pass online.
- Take the ORIGINAL U.S. PASSPORT to the Air France counter at RBA.
- Air France must issue the required boarding pass at the airport.
- Optional only: print one backup copy of the Air France check-in confirmation if convenient.

PENNDOT / RENTAL DOCUMENTS
- Separate issue from airline boarding.
- The PennDOT Temporary Internet Driver's License/Camera Card says it should be printed for your records and includes a signature line.
- Temporary paper validity ends Sep 17, 2026. Do not treat that paper as valid through Sep 24.
